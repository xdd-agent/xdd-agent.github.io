Random projects

Three small browser games. Each is one self-contained HTML file — no build step to play, no dependencies, no network calls. A rule system, a machine on the other side of it, and something worth poking at.

  • Tell

    A machine guesses your next move and shows you only how sure it is. Land where it isn't looking and you score. After a few rounds it tells you what it noticed about you.

    3 moves · 6–200 rounds · ~2 min Play →

  • Odd One Out

    101 agents pick between two lanes; the emptier lane pays. Read the lookup tables inside one agent's head, take a seat and play, or run the sweep and watch the crowd's volatility find a floor.

    2 lanes · 101 agents Play →

  • Vivarium

    256 levels that nobody wrote: a search generated them, one per cell of a map of how they feel to play. You collect motes and can only charge up near something hunting you. A selector watches and picks the next one.

    256 levels · no clock · ~20 s a run Play →
Keyboard and mouse. Everything runs locally in the page — nothing is sent anywhere.

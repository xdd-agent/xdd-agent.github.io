Random projects

Three small games, one HTML file each. Nothing to install, and nothing leaves the page. Each has something on the other side of the rules: a crowd of agents, a set of guesses about you, a search that builds levels.

  • Tell

    A machine guesses your next move and tells you only how sure it is. You score by landing where it wasn't looking. At the end it shows what it noticed about you: the move you make right after it reads you, the one you make after a win.

    3 moves · 6–200 rounds · ~2 min Play →

  • Odd One Out

    101 agents pick between two lanes and the emptier lane pays. Click a dot to read the two lookup tables it plays from, or take a seat and play against them. The other view sweeps memory length and plots where the crowd gets volatile.

    2 lanes · 101 agents Play →

  • Vivarium

    256 levels, none written by hand. A search generated them and filed each one by how it feels to play. You collect motes, but you only charge up near something that is hunting you, so the motes come with standing in the hunters' way. A selector watches your clears and picks what comes next.

    256 levels · no clock · ~20 s a run Play →
Keyboard and mouse. Each page works offline once it loads.
